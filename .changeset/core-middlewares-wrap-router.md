Middlewares replace the request hooks. `app.use(...middlewares)` wraps the router, Koa- and Hono-style: the app's middlewares run on every request, in declaration order — a 404, a 405, a preflight included — a route's chain being the middlewares declared before it, then its own, then its handler, and a request no route matches running every `use()` middleware of the app, wherever declared, then its 404 or 405 (a `use()` declared after a route runs on unmatched requests, never on that route). A group's middlewares stay with its routes. `use(path, …)` is matched against the request's path, compiled once at declaration: a `/users/:id` route requested as `/users/admin` runs `use('/users/admin', guard)`. Errors are rejections through `next()`: `validate` throws a `ValidationError` (an `HttpError<400>` carrying its `refusal`), a body past its limit a `ContentTooLargeError`, and `refusalOf(error)` reads either, so a middleware before them answers a refusal in its own format. New: `settle(ctx, next())`, the response the client would get, the route's answer to an error included, for a middleware that must see every response; `next.behind(added?)`, the rest run behind a reply sent at once. `onRequest`, `onResponse`, `around`, `wrap`, `onError`, `onRefusal` and `plugin(middleware)` are deprecated adapters, their 0.3 behaviour kept: the global ones run outside every middleware, `onError` and `onRefusal` answer at the route boundary, after every middleware. `derive`, `decorate`, `onStart`, `onStop` and `parser` stay. Breaking: `BaseContext.route` is `string | undefined` (none in a middleware on a request no route matches; a handler's is `string`), and `derive`, `decorate` and `wrap` declared on the app run on unmatched requests too. Faster: a route behind three middlewares costs about 9% more than the same three hooks in a list, from 23% (#131).
