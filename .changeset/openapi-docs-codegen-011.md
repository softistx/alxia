Docs: the generator notes describe `@nxgt/openapi-codegen` 0.11.0, the version the `api` template pins. Cookie parameters, named server-sent events and the `ignored` warnings are unchanged since 0.7.0; the guide adds that `readOnly` and `writeOnly` properties are now left out of request bodies and replies by default (`readOnly` / `writeOnly: 'keep'` restores them).
